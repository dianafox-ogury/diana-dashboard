================================================================================
                    HOW TO USE IT (5 MINUTES)
================================================================================

ACTION QUEUE:
  1. Click the "Action Queue" tab
  2. See your 3 current actions (what needs to happen)
  3. Click in "Local Notes" to add your own notes (they save automatically)
  4. Click ✓ Done when you finish something
  5. Click ⏸️ Defer to push it to later
  6. Click ✕ Dismiss to remove it from view

ACCOUNTS:
  1. Click the "Accounts" tab
  2. See all your Publicis accounts in a table
  3. Type in "Search accounts..." to find one quickly
  4. Use the "All Priorities" filter to show only Tier 1, Tier 2, etc.

PIPELINE:
  1. Click "Pipeline" tab
  2. See all your open deals with amounts, stages, and close dates
  3. Search for a deal name or filter by stage (Proposal / Negotiation / Live)

MEETINGS:
  1. Click "Meetings" tab
  2. See your upcoming client meetings with times, attendees, and agendas

IDP & GOALS:
  1. Click "IDP & Goals" tab
  2. See your promotion progress (70% to Sales Director)
  3. See your three 90-day priorities and current evidence
